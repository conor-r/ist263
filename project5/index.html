What AWS Can Do for You

Have you ever wondered how a small team can launch an app or website that can be used by millions of people without spending a fortune on infrastructure? The answer is the cloud. AWS, short for Amazon Web Services, is a cloud computing platform with more than two hundred services, including storage, computing power, databases, networking, and security. AWS's cloud computing is best described as renting computing power and other tools when you need them and paying for only what you use (AWS Overview). Instead of having to buy, set up, and maintain your own servers, you use AWS's and you can start in just minutes.

How AWS Can Save You Money

  • Pay only for what you use. AWS prices most of their services based on the resources you actually use. This lets users avoid unnecessary long term contracts or complicated licensing. AWS also offers extra discounts when you reserve capacity in advance or use more of a specific service (AWS Pricing).
  • Scale up and down as needed. Using AWS's infrastructure, you can get as much capacity as you need almost instantly so you don't have to pay for equipment that isn't used.
  • Try AWS with a free trial. AWS offers a free tier that lets you experiment with various services at no cost (AWS Free Tier).
  • No big upfront costs. Using the cloud removes the need for large up-front hardware purchases and the costs of maintaining said hardware.

Although AWS is transparent with pricing and can save you a lot of money, maximum savings aren't automatic. Resources you use and forget to turn off still cost money, so it's important to always keep an eye on your spending page and know what you're running.
